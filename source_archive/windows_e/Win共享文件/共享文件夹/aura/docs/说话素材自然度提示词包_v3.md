# AURA 说话素材自然度提示词包

版本 v3，2026 年 10 月 5 日。与 `说话素材生成与验收工作流_v3.md` 配套使用。完整且可直接构建 API 图的英文提示词保存在 `说话素材自然度提示词_v3.json`，其中 `common_prompt` 与每个条目的 `prompt` 拼接成最终提示。修改以 JSON 为准，之后用构建器生成新的批次；不要只改本说明而继续运行旧提示词。

旧 `gen_talking_variants.py` 的动作句作为历史记录保留。本轮构建器只复用它的模型及 API 图结构，不再使用其“十几次口型、抬手、两次强调、两次眨眼再放手”的动作提示。

## 统一要求

所有素材沿用同一角色、服装、身体比例、背景、相机和原始画幅。固定头、躯干和脚的位置，只允许指定的局部动作。不要让“自然”被模型解释为不断摇头、晃肩、改变体型或移动镜头。

统一英文约束如下，与 JSON 中 common_prompt 一致：

> The exact same anime girl as the provided reference images, identical face, hairstyle, outfit and body proportions. Full body standing, identical framing and constant subject scale. Static locked camera, flat unchanged lighting and exposure. A solid uniform chroma key green background matching the reference, no background shadows or gradients. Keep the head, torso, hips and feet at the same screen positions; only the specifically requested local motion is allowed. Preserve clean stable fingers and garment details. No zoom, camera motion, body resizing, walking, scene changes, extra limbs, extra objects, text, overlays, glow, particles or motion blur. Small fluent movements with gentle acceleration and deceleration, no sudden pose changes.

## P0 静息口型循环

ID `talking_calm_rest_v3`，首尾同为静息参考。优先生成这一类，手、肩和头不做强调动作。

> A calm neutral speaking loop. Both hands remain relaxed in precisely the same resting positions throughout; never raise, lower or gesture with the hands. Only the lips move in three to five small irregular open and closed cycles; closed lips recur between cycles. Maintain a friendly neutral face and open eyes, without blinking in this base clip. The head stays still; breathing is barely visible. The last frame matches the first resting pose, mouth shape and gaze. Do not perform an introduction or a recovery gesture inside this loop.

## P0 手势保持口型循环

ID `talking_calm_hold_v3`，首尾同为保持参考。要求手势已经建立，片段只负责保持和口型。

> A calm neutral speaking loop with the hand pose already established in the held reference. Keep both wrists, elbows and fingertips at those same screen positions throughout. No lifting, lowering, alternating emphasis or repeated gestures. Only the lips move in three to five small irregular speaking cycles, with brief closed-mouth intervals. Eyes stay open without blinking, gaze forward and head still. The last frame matches the first held pose and mouth shape. This is a sustained pose loop, not a whole raise-and-lower gesture.

## P0 进入片段

ID `talking_calm_enter_v3`，静息参考到保持参考。只播放一次，闭嘴，不要求回到首姿势。

> One gentle continuous transition from the first resting reference to the final held reference. Raise the requested hand pose once, following a smooth short path. Begin slowly, move calmly and settle into the final pose without overshoot. Keep the lips closed and the eyes open. No speaking, blinking, nodding, repeated emphasis or extra hand movement. The final held pose must match the final reference in wrist, elbow, finger and shoulder positions. This is a one-shot transition and must not return to the first pose.

## P0 退出片段

ID `talking_calm_exit_v3`，保持参考到静息参考。只收手一次，闭嘴，不在语音结束后继续演说话。

> One gentle continuous recovery from the first held reference to the final resting reference. Lower the hands once along a smooth short path, calmly decelerating into the final resting pose. Keep the lips closed, gaze forward and eyes open. No speaking, blinking, nodding, repeated emphasis or extra gesture. Match the final resting reference exactly in framing and hand placement. This is a one-shot recovery and must not return to the first held pose.

## P1 偶尔眨眼变体

ID `talking_calm_blink_v3`，首尾静息参考，单次眨眼。实际低频选择需后续代码实现；不应把这段作为每 1.625 秒必播一次的基础循环。

> The same calm hands-at-rest speaking loop as the base reference: hands, shoulders and head remain still, with three to five small mouth cycles and brief closed-mouth intervals. Add just one gentle natural blink near the middle, eyes open before and after. No head dip or hand gesture accompanies the blink. The last frame matches the first resting pose, mouth shape and open eyes. This variant is for occasional playback; it will not be selected on every loop.

## P1 温和愉快循环

ID `talking_warm_rest_v3`，首尾静息参考。用表情表达高情绪，先不增加大动作。

> A softly cheerful speaking loop, with a subtle warm expression rather than a broad laugh. Both hands remain at the reference resting positions, head and shoulders still. Only the mouth makes three to five modest speaking cycles, returning to a closed soft smile between cycles. Eyes stay open without blinking in this base clip. No repeated emphasis, arm lifting or bouncing torso. The last frame matches the first pose and facial expression.

## P1 低落循环

ID `talking_low_rest_v3`，首尾静息参考。不用反复低头、身体缩小或垂肩制造低落感。

> A gentle subdued speaking loop, with a mildly thoughtful calm face and no dramatic sadness. Hands remain at the reference resting positions, shoulders level, gaze forward and head still. Only the lips make three to five small speaking cycles with closed-mouth pauses. Eyes stay open without blinking in this base clip. No repeated head droop, arm movement or shrinking body. The last frame matches the first pose and expression.

## 调整提示词时的原则

每次只改一个维度。手势不稳，先去掉手部运动并检查锚点；口型太急，先减少次数和幅度；身体变大，退回固定头部、肩线和腰线的候选。不要同时要求更多动作、更多眨眼、更多表情，又期待连续性自然改善。

三到五次口型只是生成动作密度的建议，不是音素或语音对齐。真实对嘴要另做独立口型层和音频时间线。提示词要求首尾一致也不保证逐像素一致，生成后必须按工作流检查原帧、候选和跨片段接缝。
